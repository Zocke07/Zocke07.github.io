Work & Research

The Defense That Says Where It Breaks

Master's Thesis · Federated Learning Security

Federated learning keeps raw data on the device, which also means the server cannot tell a poisoned model update from an honest one. My thesis proposes L-MAD, a two-stage defense that rejects corrupted layers individually instead of discarding the whole client. It holds 97.5% DDoS-class F₁ under a 30% poisoning attack, more than 25 points clear of six reimplemented baselines, and its last chapter is built to prove my own weakest claim wrong.
